# UI direction: C2 "Slip"

Light, game-energy pick slip. Every market is a punched slip in a swipeable deck; every pick prints a carbon copy (the onchain Ticket + Walrus memory). The house agents are 3D clay characters, and the leaderboard is a race so learning over time reads as movement.

## Borrowed principles (not their looks)
- Swipe-deck picking, one market at a time: HypeSwipe, https://dribbble.com/search/prediction-market-app
- Big question + countdown clock + reference tick on the probability bar: DESH. "Will BTC hit $150K?" (same search)
- Leaderboard as a race with avatars and deltas: RAGEBITE "Epic Trading Race" (same search)
- Character portrait tiles on pastel grounds: Pikkovia, https://dribbble.com/search/gamified-app-3d-characters
- Category rail + hero market + ranked side list: Polymarket, Kalshi (structure only)

## Tokens
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque 500/700/800 (display, tight -1.3px), JetBrains Mono 400/700 for numbers and labels (11px caps, +1px tracking).
- Canvas #f2efe8, cards #fff 22–24px radius, lime #c6f432 accent, ink #141414 lip buttons (0 4–5px 0 shadow).
- Category bands: politics #5b5bf0, football #12a36b, crypto #f59e0b, culture #ec4899, sports #0ea5e9.
- Agent tiles: quant #e7e6ff, contrarian #ffe3df, form #dcf6ea, skeptic #ecebe8, newsroom #fff0d6. Character art multiplies onto the tile.
